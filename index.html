How pickup works

Three steps from browsing to collecting your meal.

  1. Browse and search

    Use the search box to look for a dish by name, or filter by category — ulam, noodles, rice and sides, snacks, sweets or drinks.

  2. Build your cart

    Add the dishes you want and adjust the quantity in your cart. Sold-out dishes are blocked from being ordered.

  3. Place a demo order

    Leave your pickup details, submit the order and track its status — Pending, Preparing, Ready for Pickup, then Completed.
