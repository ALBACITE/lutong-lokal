Admin demo area

Manage the demo menu, dish availability and the demo orders that come in.

Opening this page does not grant you access. The administrator check in this prototype happens in your browser, so anybody can open the page or edit browser storage to get past it. That is exactly why it is a demonstration and not real security — a real system would enforce this on a trusted server.
