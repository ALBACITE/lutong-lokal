Create a demo customer account

Registering gives you a customer account so you can keep your demo orders together in one place.

Demo registration is not a secure sign-up. The account is created inside your browser and is visible to anybody who uses the same browser profile. Do not enter real personal information, and never use a password you use elsewhere.

What this prototype stores

Only what is needed to show the feature working:

Your name
Shown on your dashboard and on your demo orders.
Username
Used to sign in to the demo site.
Email address
Used to sign in to the demo site. Please use an address you control.

No passwords are collected. The demo sign-in uses a public demo passcode instead of a password, so there is nothing sensitive to store. A real version of this site would need a trusted backend or an authentication provider with server-side access rules, hashed passwords and secure sessions.
